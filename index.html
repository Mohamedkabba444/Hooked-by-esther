Hi, I'm Esther

I picked up my first crochet hook to make a blanket for a friend's baby, and I haven't put it down since. Now I crochet, knit and sew small-batch pieces at my kitchen table, usually with a cup of tea going cold beside me.

I use soft, durable yarns and take my time on every stitch, because handmade things should last. If you can imagine it, we can usually make it.

Looking after handmade crochet

A little care keeps your piece soft and in shape for years.

Washing

Hand wash in cool water with a gentle detergent. Press the water out. Never wring or twist.

Machine wash

Only if your piece is labelled machine washable. Use a mesh bag, a wool or delicate cycle and cold water.

Drying

Lay flat on a clean towel and reshape gently. Keep away from direct heat and sunlight. No tumble dryer.

Storing

Fold and store somewhere dry. Avoid hanging heavy blankets and bags, as they can stretch.
